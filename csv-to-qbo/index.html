CSV to QBO Converter

QuickBooks Desktop will not import a CSV into a bank account. It wants a QBO (Web Connect) file. Drop your CSV or Excel export here and get a QBO file that imports like a normal bank download.

🔒 Your file is read in your browser and never uploaded. The first 10 transactions are free; FileFreely Pro unlocks full files.

How to convert CSV or Excel to QBO

  1. Drop your file in the box above. The converter finds the date, description and amount columns.
  2. Check the column choices and the preview. Fix the date format or flip signs if needed.
  3. Choose the output and account type, then download.

Import it into QuickBooks Desktop

  1. In QuickBooks Desktop, open the bank or credit card account you want to fill. If it is connected to a live bank feed, deactivate the feed first (Banking > Bank Feeds > Bank Feeds Center).
  2. Go to File > Utilities > Import > Web Connect Files and choose the .qbo file.
  3. When asked, pick Use an existing QuickBooks account and select the account. QuickBooks shows the bank name from the Bank ID (Wells Fargo for the default 3000). That label does not matter.
  4. Review and add the transactions in the Bank Feeds Center.

Why use this converter

Banks let you download transactions as CSV, but the banking import in QuickBooks Desktop only accepts Web Connect files (.qbo). Typing hundreds of lines by hand is slow and error-prone. This converter reads the date, description and amount columns from your CSV, writes them into the OFX-based QBO format QuickBooks expects, and adds a unique transaction ID to each line so QuickBooks can spot duplicates.

It works with exports from Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Capital One, Amex, PayPal, Stripe and most other banks and card issuers, including files with separate debit and credit columns.

Frequently asked questions

Why does QuickBooks say the file is from Wells Fargo?

QBO files carry a Bank ID (INTU.BID). QuickBooks only accepts IDs from banks it knows, and 3000 (Wells Fargo) is accepted for any bank. The label only appears during import. Your transactions go into whichever account you pick.

Can I import into an account that already has a bank feed?

QuickBooks Desktop links a Web Connect file to an account by its account number and Bank ID. If the account is connected to a live feed for a different bank, deactivate the feed, import the file, then reconnect.

My CSV has separate debit and credit columns. Will that work?

Yes. Choose "Separate debit and credit columns" and pick both columns. Debits become money out and credits money in.

Is my bank data uploaded?

No. The file is read and converted by your browser. Nothing is sent to FileFreely or anyone else.
