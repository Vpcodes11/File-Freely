How to use HEIC to JPG

  1. Drop your .heic photos in the box. You can add many at once.
  2. Keep quality at 92 for near-original results.
  3. Click Convert to JPG and download them, or all at once as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

What is a HEIC file?

HEIC is the photo format iPhones use by default since iOS 11. It saves space but isn't supported everywhere.

Will I lose quality?

At the default setting of 92, the difference is not visible.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Conversion runs in your browser, so your photos stay private.
