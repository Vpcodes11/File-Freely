Which format do I need?

QuickBooks Desktop: QBO for bank-feed style imports, IIF for foreign-currency accounts or older versions. QuickBooks Online: the 3-column CSV or OFX. Quicken: QFX. Xero, Sage, FreeAgent, YNAB: OFX or the Xero CSV. Banktivity, GnuCash, Moneydance: QIF or OFX.
