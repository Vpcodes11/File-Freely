American Express Statement to QuickBooks Converter

Download your transactions from American Express, drop the file here and get a file QuickBooks imports cleanly. The converter knows the American Express export layout, so the date, payee and amount columns are picked for you.

🔒 Your file is read in your browser and never uploaded. The first 10 transactions are free; full files start at $5.

1. Download your transactions from American Express

Sign in at americanexpress.com, open Statements & Activity, choose the period, then Download and pick CSV.

2. Convert the American Express file

Amex CSV files list charges as positive numbers and payments and credits as negative numbers, the opposite of what accounting files expect. This page turns on Flip signs for you, so charges import as money spent. Check the preview: a purchase should show as a negative amount.

  1. Drop the file in the box above.
  2. Check the preview: dates, payees and amounts should match your statement.
  3. Choose the output (QuickBooks is selected; QBO, QFX, OFX, IIF and QIF are also available) and download.

3. Import it into QuickBooks Desktop

  1. In QuickBooks Desktop, open the bank or credit card account you want to fill. If it is connected to a live bank feed, deactivate the feed first (Banking > Bank Feeds > Bank Feeds Center).
  2. Go to File > Utilities > Import > Web Connect Files and choose the .qbo file.
  3. When asked, pick Use an existing QuickBooks account and select the account. QuickBooks shows the bank name from the Bank ID (Wells Fargo for the default 3000). That label does not matter.
  4. Review and add the transactions in the Bank Feeds Center.

Frequently asked questions

My Amex file has extra columns like Extended Details and Address. Is that a problem?

No. The converter picks the Date, Description and Amount columns and ignores the rest.

Can I import an Amex statement PDF?

Yes. Drop the PDF in the box. Keep Flip signs on if the preview shows charges as positive.

Is my bank data uploaded?

No. The file is read and converted by your browser. Nothing is sent to FileFreely or anyone else.

How much does it cost?

The preview and the first 10 transactions of every file are free. Full files cost $5 for one file (24 hours), $9 for a 7-day pass or $19 a month.
