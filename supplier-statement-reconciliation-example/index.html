Supplier Statement Reconciliation Example

To reconcile a supplier statement in Excel, compare the supplier’s open items with your accounts payable ledger as of the same date. Match invoice references and signed amounts, then review any missing invoices, duplicate references, and credits before updating the ledger.

This fictional October example gives you both inputs and the finished four-sheet workbook.

  • Download the supplier statement PDF Two pages, five transactions
  • Download the AP ledger CSV Five rows, including a duplicate reference
  • Download the finished Excel report Summary, Exceptions, Matched, and Source Rows

What the reconciliation found

Expected results for Sample Supply Co.
ReferenceResultWhat to check
INV-00104Amount mismatchSupplier shows $420 on page 1. Ledger line 2 shows $400. The signed difference is +$20.
INV-00105MatchedThe unique reference and signed amount of $185 agree.
INV-00106Missing in ledgerSupplier lists $260. Check whether the invoice arrived or was posted after cutoff.
INV-00107Duplicate reviewTwo ledger lines show $110. Review both entries before confirming a match.
CR-00108Credit reviewSupplier shows a $25 credit. Check whether it was posted or applied to another invoice.
INV-00109Missing on statementLedger shows $90. Check statement timing or payment allocation.

The source totals are $950 on the statement and $895 in the ledger, a signed difference of +$55. That total includes the unresolved duplicate and credit. Use the Exceptions and Source Rows sheets to see the reason and location for each item.

How to reproduce the report

  1. Open Reconcile and choose the sample PDF as the supplier statement and the CSV as the accounts payable ledger.
  2. Preview PDF pages 1–2 with header row 2. Preview the CSV with header row 1.
  3. Set supplier to Sample Supply Co., currency to USD, and cutoff to October 31, 2026. Select Open items, Outstanding balance, and My exports are already filtered.
  4. Confirm the Reference, Amount, Date, and Type mappings on both sides. Keep the signs as shown. Run reconciliation and download the Excel report.
