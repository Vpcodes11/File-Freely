How to use Resize Image

  1. Drop your images in the box.
  2. Enter a new width and height, or choose a percentage.
  3. Click Resize images to download.

Frequently asked questions

What does 'Keep proportions' do?

It changes height automatically when you change width, so the image isn't stretched.

What happens with several images of different shapes?

With proportions kept, each image is resized to your width and its own matching height.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Resizing happens in your browser.

If your images also need a common output format or filename pattern, use the batch image resizer with renaming and WebP export.
