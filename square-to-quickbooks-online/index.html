Square Statement to QuickBooks Online Converter

Download your transactions from Square, drop the file here and get a file QuickBooks Online imports cleanly. The converter knows the Square export layout, so the date, payee and amount columns are picked for you.

🔒 Your file is read in your browser and never uploaded. The first 10 transactions are free; full files start at $5.

1. Download your transactions from Square

In the Square Dashboard, go to Transactions, set the date range and choose Export > Transactions CSV.

2. Convert the Square file

Square transaction exports have dozens of columns, including Gross Sales, Discounts, Tax, Tip, Total Collected, Fees and Net Total. The converter uses Net Total (what reached your balance after Square fees) as the amount and the Description as the payee.

  1. Drop the file in the box above.
  2. Check the preview: dates, payees and amounts should match your statement.
  3. Choose the output (QuickBooks Online is selected; QBO, QFX, OFX, IIF and QIF are also available) and download.

3. Import it into QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, go to Transactions > Bank transactions.
  2. Open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file, then pick the CSV.
  3. Choose the account, confirm the column mapping (Date, Description, Amount) and the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
  4. Review the transactions in the For review tab.

Frequently asked questions

I want gross sales and fees as separate lines.

Pick Total Collected as the amount column for the sales, and record fees from the Fees column as a separate expense.

Does the file include payouts to my bank?

Payouts are listed separately in Square under Balance. Match deposits in your bank account to Square sales as transfers.

Is my bank data uploaded?

No. The file is read and converted by your browser. Nothing is sent to FileFreely or anyone else.

How much does it cost?

The preview and the first 10 transactions of every file are free. Full files cost $5 for one file (24 hours), $9 for a 7-day pass or $19 a month.
