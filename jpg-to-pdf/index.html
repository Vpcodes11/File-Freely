How to use JPG to PDF

  1. Drop your images in the box. JPG, PNG, WebP and HEIC all work.
  2. Reorder them with the arrows and choose a page size.
  3. Click Create PDF to download.

Frequently asked questions

Which page size should I choose?

US Letter is standard in the US. Choose 'Same as image' if you want each page to match the photo exactly.

Can I convert iPhone photos?

Yes. HEIC photos from iPhone are converted automatically.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.

For a smaller photo PDF, resize your images in bulk before combining them. If the finished document still exceeds a portal’s upload limit, compress the PDF to that size.
