How to convert a bank statement to Excel

Download your statement as a PDF from your online banking, not a scan or photo. Drop it in the box above. The converter is already set to combine repeating statement tables, so transactions from every page end up in one continuous table. Check the header row number in the preview: it should point to the row with column names such as Date, Description, Debit, Credit and Balance. Then download Excel for analysis or CSV for importing into accounting software.

Why your statement stays private

Bank statements contain account numbers, balances and spending history. Many online converters upload them to a server for processing. FileFreely reads the PDF inside your browser using open-source libraries, builds the spreadsheet on your device and never sends the file anywhere. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works.

Getting clean columns

Banks lay out statements differently. If a description spills into the amount column, switch the column gap setting to small or large and extract again. If page totals, running headers or promotional text appear as rows, delete them in Excel or filter them out after import. Credit card statements often show payments as negative amounts or in a separate credits column. Check the sign convention your accounting software expects before importing.

Importing into QuickBooks, Xero or a budget spreadsheet

Most accounting tools accept a CSV with a date, a description and either a single amount column or separate debit and credit columns. After downloading, open the CSV, make sure dates are in one consistent format, remove opening and closing balance rows, and save. Then use the bank statement or transactions import option and map the columns. For a budget, the Excel download is easier: add a category column and use a pivot table to total spending by month.

Frequently asked questions

Which bank statements does this work with?

It works with digital PDF statements from most banks and card issuers, including Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Citi, Capital One, American Express, Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds and NatWest, as long as the PDF has selectable text. Statements downloaded from online banking usually do.

Is it safe to convert my bank statement here?

Your statement is processed entirely in your browser and is never uploaded to a server. That makes it safer than converters that ask you to upload financial documents.

Can I convert a scanned or photographed statement?

Not directly. Scans and photos are images without a text layer. Run them through OCR first, or download the original PDF from your online banking.

How do I import the result into QuickBooks or Xero?

Download the CSV, check that the date, description and amount columns are correct, then use your accounting software's bank statement import. Most accept a CSV with date, description and amount columns.

Why are some columns split or merged?

Statements use different spacing. Try the column gap setting, check the header row number, and review the preview before downloading.
