How to convert PDF statement to IIF

  1. Drop your file in the box above. The converter finds the date, description and amount columns.
  2. Check the column choices and the preview. Fix the date format or flip signs if needed.
  3. Choose the output and account type, then download.

Import it into QuickBooks Desktop (IIF)

  1. Back up your company file first. IIF imports cannot be undone with one click.
  2. Make sure the account name you typed above matches your chart of accounts exactly, including any parent account (for example Bank:Checking).
  3. In QuickBooks Desktop go to File > Utilities > Import > IIF Files and choose the .iif file.
  4. Every line posts against the offset account (Ask My Accountant by default). Recode those lines to the right expense or income accounts afterwards.

Why use this converter

For foreign-currency accounts, older QuickBooks versions, or accounts where Web Connect keeps failing, IIF is the dependable route into QuickBooks Desktop. This tool reads the PDF in your browser and writes checks and deposits (or credit card charges and refunds) ready to import.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it for a credit card statement?

Yes. Set the account type to Credit card. Charges become CREDIT CARD transactions and refunds CCARD REFUND.

Should I back up first?

Yes. IIF imports cannot be undone with one click.

Does it read scanned PDFs?

No. Use a digital statement from online banking.

Is my statement uploaded?

No.
