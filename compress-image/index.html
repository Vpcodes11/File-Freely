How to use Compress Image

  1. Drop one or many images in the box.
  2. Set the quality. 70–80 keeps photos looking sharp at a fraction of the size.
  3. Click Compress images and download them one by one or as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

What quality setting should I use?

Start at 75. Lower it if you need a smaller file, raise it if you see blurriness.

Should I choose JPG or WebP?

JPG works everywhere. WebP is usually 25–35% smaller and works in all modern browsers, but some older apps can't open it.

Why did a file stay the same size?

If an image is already well optimized, we keep the original rather than give you a bigger file.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Compression happens in your browser.
