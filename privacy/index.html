Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Your files

FileFreely's tools run entirely in your web browser. Files you select are processed on your device and are not uploaded to, stored on, or viewed by our servers.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are visited, how long people stay, browser and device type, and approximate location (country or city). Google Analytics uses cookies. Visitors in the UK, EU and EEA are asked for consent first, and analytics cookies are not set unless you accept. We use this only to improve the site. You can opt out at any time with Google\'s browser add-on.

Layout setup requests

Netlify Forms stores the email address and file format choices you submit so we can respond to your request. Please keep your documents and financial details out of the request. Reconcile and its setup page offer separate analytics controls. When enabled, analytics records the page view in an isolated frame.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads from third-party vendors, including Google. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser; the tools still work. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have rights to access or delete personal information we hold. Contact us to make a request.

Children

FileFreely is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows when it last changed.
