How to convert CSV or Excel to QIF

  1. Drop your file in the box above. The converter finds the date, description and amount columns.
  2. Check the column choices and the preview. Fix the date format or flip signs if needed.
  3. Choose the output and account type, then download.

Import it into Quicken, Banktivity, GnuCash and older apps

  1. Open the app and use its QIF import, usually File > Import > QIF.
  2. Choose the account to import into and confirm the date format (QIF dates are written as MM/DD/YYYY).
  3. Quicken for Windows limits QIF imports into bank and credit card accounts. If it refuses the file, convert to QFX instead.
  4. Check the register and reconcile against your statement.

Why use this converter

Many personal finance apps still import QIF more reliably than CSV. This converter writes bank or credit card QIF files with dates in MM/DD/YYYY, payee, amount, memo and check number. For Quicken for Windows bank accounts, QFX is usually the better choice.

Frequently asked questions

Which apps import QIF?

Quicken (with limits for bank accounts on Windows), Banktivity, GnuCash, Moneydance, MYOB and many older apps.

Quicken will not accept my QIF file. Why?

Quicken for Windows restricts QIF imports into bank and credit card accounts. Use our CSV to QFX converter instead.

What date format does QIF use?

MM/DD/YYYY, which is what this tool writes.
