How to use Merge PDF

  1. Drop your PDF files in the box, or click to choose them.
  2. Use the ↑ and ↓ buttons to put them in the right order.
  3. Click Merge PDFs. Your combined file downloads automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?

No. There's no daily limit and no cap on the number of files. Very large files depend only on your device's memory.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. Merging happens inside your browser, so your documents never leave your device.

Will merging add a watermark?

No. The merged PDF is exactly your pages, with nothing added.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Not yet. Remove the password in the original app first, then merge.

If the combined file exceeds an attachment limit, compress the PDF to 500 KB, 1 MB, or a custom target. If you are merging a revised set of pages, compare the new PDF with the original to check the page order and edits.
