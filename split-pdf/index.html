How to use Split PDF

  1. Drop your PDF in the box.
  2. Choose Extract pages and type the pages to keep (like 1-3, 5), or choose to save every page separately.
  3. Click Split PDF and download your files.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write page ranges?

Separate pages with commas and use a dash for ranges. For example, 1-3, 5, 8-10 keeps pages 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 9 and 10.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Splitting happens in your browser and the file never leaves your device.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no daily limits, no sign-up and no watermark.
