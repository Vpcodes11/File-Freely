How to convert PDF statement to QBO

  1. Drop your file in the box above. The converter finds the date, description and amount columns.
  2. Check the column choices and the preview. Fix the date format or flip signs if needed.
  3. Choose the output and account type, then download.

Import it into QuickBooks Desktop

  1. In QuickBooks Desktop, open the bank or credit card account you want to fill. If it is connected to a live bank feed, deactivate the feed first (Banking > Bank Feeds > Bank Feeds Center).
  2. Go to File > Utilities > Import > Web Connect Files and choose the .qbo file.
  3. When asked, pick Use an existing QuickBooks account and select the account. QuickBooks shows the bank name from the Bank ID (Wells Fargo for the default 3000). That label does not matter.
  4. Review and add the transactions in the Bank Feeds Center.

Why use this converter

Banks often only keep older months as PDF statements, and QuickBooks cannot import a PDF. This tool finds each line that starts with a date and ends with an amount, joins descriptions that wrap onto a second line, and skips page headers and balance lines. You check the result in the preview before downloading.

It needs a digital PDF with selectable text, which is what online banking provides. Scanned paper statements need OCR first.

Frequently asked questions

Which PDF statements work?

Digital statements from online banking for most US, UK, Canadian and Australian banks and card issuers. If you can select the text in your PDF viewer, it should work.

Some amounts are in the wrong column. What do I do?

Statements with separate withdrawal and deposit columns are read as Amount 1 and Amount 2. Choose "Separate debit and credit columns" and assign them, or flip signs if spending shows as positive.

Does it read scanned statements?

No. Scans are images. Download the PDF from your online banking instead.

Is my statement uploaded?

No. The PDF is read inside your browser.
