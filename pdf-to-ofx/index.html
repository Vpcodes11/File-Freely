How to convert PDF statement to OFX

  1. Drop your file in the box above. The converter finds the date, description and amount columns.
  2. Check the column choices and the preview. Fix the date format or flip signs if needed.
  3. Choose the output and account type, then download.

Import it into Xero, QuickBooks Online, Sage, YNAB and other apps

  1. Xero: Accounting > Bank accounts > choose the account > Manage account > Import a statement, then upload the .ofx file.
  2. QuickBooks Online: Transactions > Bank transactions > Link account drop-down > Upload from file, then choose the .ofx file and the account.
  3. YNAB, Sage, MoneyMoney, Banktivity: use the file import option on the account and pick the .ofx file.
  4. Review the imported lines before reconciling. Each line has a unique ID, so importing the same file twice is easy to spot.

Why use this converter

Missing months in your bank feed are usually available only as PDF statements. This converter reads every page, finds the transaction lines and writes an OFX file in the currency you choose, ready for Xero, QuickBooks Online, Sage, FreeAgent or YNAB.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with UK bank statements?

Yes. Day/month/year dates are detected automatically, and you can choose GBP as the currency.

What if paid-out and paid-in are separate columns?

Choose "Separate debit and credit columns" and assign Amount 1 and Amount 2.

Does it read scanned statements?

No. It needs a digital PDF with selectable text.

Is my statement uploaded?

No.
