Frequently asked questions

Which format should I use?

JPG for photos that need to work everywhere, PNG for graphics and screenshots or when you need transparency, WebP for the smallest files on the web.

Does converting to JPG remove transparency?

Yes. JPG doesn't support transparency, so transparent areas become white. Choose PNG or WebP to keep it.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
